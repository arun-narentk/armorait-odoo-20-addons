Purchase Vendor Previous Price

See what you paid this vendor last time, directly on purchase order lines.

Overview

When a buyer selects a product on a Purchase Order line, the module shows the most recent confirmed purchase price for that vendor and product, plus the date of that purchase. Unit Price stays untouched.

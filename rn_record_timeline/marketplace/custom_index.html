Overview

Pipeline, engagement, and customer follow-up in Odoo

Answer "What happened to this record?" with a visual GitHub-style timeline.

Record Timeline Supported documents: sale orders, purchase orders, invoices, CRM leads It is built for Odoo 20 Community by ARMORA IT Technologies.

The Problem

  • Leads and follow-ups slip between email and WhatsApp
  • Pipeline visibility is weak for managers
  • Engagement history is incomplete on the partner
  • No clear dashboard for today's sales work
  • Teams invent their own CRM process per user

The Solution

  • CRM workflows centered on Odoo opportunities
  • Engagement and activity tracking
  • Smart buttons to related customer records
  • Sales dashboards for open pipeline
  • Consistent process with access rights

Installation

  1. Add rn_record_timeline to your addons path
  2. Restart the Odoo service
  3. Apps → Update Apps List
  4. Search Record Timeline
  5. Click Install
  6. Complete settings and use the Quotes smart workflows

# Construction ERP Core Roadmap

## Phase 1 (current)

- Projects, sites, BOQ, estimation, site logs, procurement, labour, equipment, billing
- Dashboards and heuristic AI insights

## Phase 2

- Mobile site app with offline sync
- Subcontractor portal and RA bill automation
- Drawing and document search
- Deeper stock consumption at site level

## Phase 3

- LLM project assistant and drawing Q&A
- Predictive material planning from historical projects
- Integration packs for WhatsApp site updates and OCR on delivery challans

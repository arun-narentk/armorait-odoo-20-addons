# Rental Management

        Assets, pricing, availability, and bookings for any rental business

        ## Business problem
        Rental businesses juggle spreadsheets for asset availability, pricing seasons, and double bookings.

        ## Solution
        Rental Management is an industry-agnostic rental engine with assets, pricing rules, conflict detection, and an OWL operations dashboard.

        ## Key benefits
        - Stop double bookings
- Flexible pricing rules
- Asset lifecycle tracking
- Portal-ready foundation
- Extend with industry packs

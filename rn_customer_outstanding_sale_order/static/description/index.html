Customer Outstanding on Sale Order

See how much the customer currently owes, right on the Sale Order.

Overview

Displays the customer receivable outstanding balance next to the Customer field. The amount uses Odoo Total Receivable from posted accounting entries.

Customer: ABC Customer

Customer Outstanding: 125,000.00

Key Features

  • Outstanding amount on Sale Order form
  • Posted receivables only
  • Invoices, credit notes, partial and full payments
  • Commercial partner and multi-company support
  • Visual warning when outstanding is greater than zero
  • No configuration menus

Compatibility

Odoo 20 Community · Depends on Sale and Accounting · License OPL-1

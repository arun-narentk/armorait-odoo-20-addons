# Veterinary ERP Core

ARMORA IT Technologies veterinary clinic platform for Odoo 20 Community.

Focused niche with lower competition in India. Companion animal clinics and pet hospitals.

## Phase 1 features

- Pet and owner management (multiple pets per owner)
- Appointments: consultation, vaccination, surgery, grooming, boarding, emergency
- Vaccination schedules with due dates and booster intervals
- Medical records, prescriptions, lab results, imaging attachments
- Surgery scheduling and recovery notes
- Grooming services
- Boarding: kennels, check-in/out, feeding schedules
- Clinic owner and veterinarian OWL dashboards
- AI hooks: clinical notes, vaccination assistant, reminders, analytics

## Why Veterinary ERP

Narrower than hospital ERP. Pet-owner relationship, vaccinations, boarding, and grooming are poorly modeled in generic ERPs.

## Installation

1. Install Accounting, HR, Inventory, Website, Portal.
2. Install **Veterinary ERP Core**.
3. Assign Reception, Veterinarian, or Clinic Manager groups.

## Ecosystem

Works with AI Copilot, Workflow Builder, WhatsApp, document platform, and Invoice OCR when installed.

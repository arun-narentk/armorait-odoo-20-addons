# AI School OS Roadmap

## Phase 1 (current)

- Students, admissions, fees, attendance, timetable
- Exams, homework, notices
- Principal and teacher dashboards
- AI comments, risk detection, admission assistant

## Phase 2

- Parent portal (attendance, fees, homework, report cards)
- Online admission forms on website
- WhatsApp fee reminders and absence alerts
- QR attendance kiosk

## Phase 3

- Transport GPS and pickup notifications
- Library and hostel modules
- Mobile app companion

## Phase 4

- LLM timetable optimization
- AI parent communication hub
- Multi-campus analytics
- Specialty packs (dental school, coaching center)

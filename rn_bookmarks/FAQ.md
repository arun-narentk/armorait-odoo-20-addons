# FAQ

## Can I bookmark any model?

Yes. Record bookmarks work on any model through `base` inheritance. Add the toggle widget to custom form views for best UX.

## Are bookmarks shared with my team?

Version 1 is private per user. Team sharing is planned for v2.

## What is the difference between pin and favorite?

**Pinned** items appear at the top of the sidebar. **Favorites** are a separate filter under Bookmarks menu.

## Can I bookmark a filtered list?

Yes. Use **Bookmarks > Create Bookmark**, choose **List View**, set model and domain.

## Does Ctrl+B work everywhere?

It works on form views when the hotkey is enabled in settings and the controller exposes `resModel` and `resId`.

## Can I bookmark a menu or report?

Yes. Use the Create Bookmark wizard with type **Menu** or **Report**.

## What happens if I delete the original record?

The bookmark remains but opening it may show a missing record. Clean up stale bookmarks from **All Bookmarks**.

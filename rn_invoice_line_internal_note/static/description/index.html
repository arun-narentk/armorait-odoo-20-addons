Invoice Line Internal Note

Line-level notes for invoices and bills that stay internal.

Overview

Adds an Internal Note on each invoice or bill line. Notes are for internal users only and are not printed on the standard customer or vendor PDF.

Key Features

  • Per-line Internal Note on invoices and bills
  • Customer invoices, credit notes, vendor bills, refunds
  • Not shown on PDF / portal / email templates
  • No effect on totals or accounting

Compatibility

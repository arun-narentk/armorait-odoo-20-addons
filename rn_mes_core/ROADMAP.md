# Manufacturing MES Roadmap

## Phase 1 (current)

- Tablet execution UI
- Work order lifecycle on shop floor
- Barcode hooks
- Quality inspections
- Downtime tracking
- OEE snapshots
- IoT device registry (manual/MQTT/OPC UA ready)

## Phase 2

- Drag-and-drop scheduling board
- Shift management and handover notes
- Digital SOPs (images, PDFs, videos)
- Tool life and calibration tracking
- Deeper `mrp` stock move integration on complete

## Phase 3

- IoT gateway service (MQTT, Modbus, OPC UA)
- PLC/CNC connectors (Siemens, Fanuc, Allen-Bradley)
- Live machine monitoring and energy tracking
- Automated downtime from alarms

## Phase 4

- AI production assistant (root cause, line miss analysis)
- Predictive maintenance recommendations
- AI scrap pattern detection
- AI scheduling suggestions

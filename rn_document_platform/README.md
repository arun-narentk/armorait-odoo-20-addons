# Digital Document Platform

ARMORA IT Technologies enterprise document workflow for Odoo 20 Community.

Sign, approve, audit, and AI-analyze contracts and business documents.

## Phase 1

- Multi-signer sign requests (sequential and parallel)
- PDF attachment and signature field placement
- Email invitations and public signing portal
- Audit trail with IP, user agent, document hash
- AI contract summary and risk flags
- Integration with sales orders, purchase orders, HR contracts
- SaaS signature credits and dashboard

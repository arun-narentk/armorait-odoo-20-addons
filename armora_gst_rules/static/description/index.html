Frequently asked questions

Q.Which Odoo versions and editions does it support?
Odoo 20.0, both Community and Enterprise. It works on your own server and on Odoo.sh.
Q.What do I need to install first?
You need Base (Free). Odoo installs it for you when you install this app, and the Odoo Apps store sells it with this app, so you pay nothing in total. It also uses the Odoo apps Invoicing, Indian - Accounting.
Q.Does this app make my business GST compliant by itself?
No. It helps your team check and track GST data inside Odoo. Your tax advisor and the GST Portal stay the final reference.
Q.Which license is it under?
OPL-1 (Odoo Proprietary License v1.0). You can use and change it for your own company. You cannot resell or share the code.
Q.Can you set it up or change it for our business?
Yes. ARMORA IT Technologies does Odoo setup, GST app changes and support from Bengaluru. Write to info@armorait.com.

Installation

  1. Copy armora_gst_rules and its dependencies to your addons path
  2. Restart the Odoo service
  3. Turn on developer mode, then go to Apps and click Update Apps List
  4. Search for Armora GST Rules
  5. Click Activate

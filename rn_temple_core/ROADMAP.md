# Temple Digital Platform Roadmap

## Phase 1 (current)

- Devotees, donations, sevas, festivals, annadhanam, hundi, volunteers, assets
- Trustee and office dashboards
- Heuristic AI assistant hooks

## Phase 2

- Devotee mobile app: book sevas, donate, download receipts
- Online seva booking on website
- Multi-language: Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Hindi, English
- E-hundi and donation campaigns

## Phase 3

- Hall booking, prasadam shop, membership programs
- LLM temple copilot and drawing/document archive
- Multi-temple trust hierarchy and custom branding

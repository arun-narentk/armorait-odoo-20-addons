# User Guide

## Create a promotion

1. Go to **Website > AI BIZ Promotions > Promotions**
2. Click New
3. Fill title, subtitle, description, footer text, CTA
4. Upload banner image
5. Add benefits on the Benefits tab
6. Set Active and Sequence

## Publish

Open `/ai-biz` on the website. The lowest-sequence active promotion is shown.

## Tips

- Keep benefit titles short
- Use square icons for best card layout
- Put the primary campaign at sequence 10

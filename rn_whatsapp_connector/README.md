# WhatsApp Automation

        Send invoices, orders, and alerts on WhatsApp from Odoo 20

        ## Business problem
        Teams chase customers on email and phone while invoices, deliveries, and payment updates sit unread in Odoo.

        ## Solution
        WhatsApp Automation connects Odoo to WhatsApp Business providers, queues messages, and triggers notifications from sales, invoices, and deliveries.

        ## Key benefits
        - Reach customers on WhatsApp
- Automate invoice and order updates
- Queue and retry failed messages
- Multi-provider adapter framework
- OWL dashboard with live KPIs

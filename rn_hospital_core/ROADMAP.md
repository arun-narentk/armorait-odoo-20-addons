# Hospital ERP Core Roadmap

## Phase 1 (current)

- Patient management and UHID
- Appointments and OPD queue
- EMR encounters and prescriptions
- IPD wards, beds, admissions
- Billing hooks to accounting
- Doctor and hospital dashboards
- AI scribe/discharge drafts (heuristic)

## Phase 2

- Pharmacy module (dispensing, batch, expiry)
- Laboratory orders and results
- Radiology reports
- Online patient portal booking
- SMS/WhatsApp reminders

## Phase 3

- Operation theatre scheduling
- Insurance and TPA billing
- Nursing vitals and medication administration
- Package billing

## Phase 4

- Specialty verticals: `rn_hospital_dental`, `rn_hospital_eye`
- LLM medical scribe and prescription assistant
- Patient WhatsApp assistant
- Device and HL7/FHIR integration layer

## Entry strategy

Start with specialty clinics before full multi-specialty hospital rollout.
